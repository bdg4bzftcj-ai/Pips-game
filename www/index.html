How to play

Cover every square with the tiles in your tray. Each tile covers two neighbouring squares. Every tile gets used.

Coloured regions carry a rule. When the board is full, every rule has to hold.

  • 7 The spots in the region add up to exactly 7.
  • <4 The total is less than 4.
  • >9 The total is more than 9.
  • = Every square shows the same number.
  • ≠ Every square shows a different number.
  • · Grey squares have no rule.

A new easy, medium and hard puzzle comes out every day. Everything back to 1 January 2025 is in the archive.
